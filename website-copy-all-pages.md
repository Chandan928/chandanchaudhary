### Section 3 — How I think

**Eyebrow:** My approach

**H2:**
SEO is an execution problem,
not a knowledge problem.

Most companies know *what* to do. They have the audits. They have the keyword lists. What they don't have is a system that turns that knowledge into consistent, compounding organic growth.

My job is to build that system — and then make sure it runs without me.

**Five principles I work by:**

1. **Revenue first, always.** Every SEO decision I make gets evaluated against one question: does this move ARR? Rankings are a side effect of doing that well.

2. **AI is a multiplier, not a replacement.** I use AI to move faster — smarter content clustering, automated reporting, programmatic systems. But strategy and judgment are still human.

3. **Technical foundations are non-negotiable.** You can have the best content strategy in your industry and still lose if your crawl architecture is broken.

4. **Compounding beats sprinting.** The best SEO systems are the ones that get stronger over time. That means building for 12 months from day one.

5. **Transparency is the only retainer worth having.** I'd rather tell you the honest timeline than keep you on a contract you're not getting value from.

**Q: How is this different from hiring an SEO agency?**
A: Agencies spread their best people thin across 20+ clients. When you work with me, you get one person — senior, focused, and accountable for outcomes. I don't hand your account to a junior analyst. Every strategy, every decision, every deliverable comes from me.

**Q: How long before I see results?**
A: For technical SEO and on-page work — 6 to 10 weeks for measurable movement. For content strategy and topical authority — 3 to 6 months for compounding results. I'll be honest with you from day one about what's realistic for your specific situation.

**Q: Do I need a big team to work with you?**
A: No. I've worked with solo founders and teams of 50+. I adapt to your resources. I can work independently or alongside your existing marketing team. I'll use your internal resources where it makes sense and flag where you might need to hire.

**Q: What industries do you specialize in?**
A: SaaS is my primary focus — especially B2B SaaS, EdTech, and HR tech. I've also worked extensively in e-commerce, OTT, healthcare, and consumer apps. The principles are consistent. The execution is always tailored to your specific market.

**Q: Do you require long-term contracts?**
A: The Clarity Sprint is a one-time engagement — no strings attached. For ongoing work, I ask for a 3-month minimum so we have enough time to build momentum and show measurable results. After that, you can continue or stop with 30 days notice.

**Q: Do you work with international clients?**
A: Yes. I've worked remotely with teams in the US, UAE, Canada, and across Europe. Time zones have never been a problem — I'm responsive and async-friendly.

**Q: What does the onboarding process look like?**
A: After we book a call, I spend the first two weeks doing a deep audit of your current organic performance, business model, and competitive landscape. Week three, I present a prioritized 90-day roadmap. Week four, we start executing. No wasted time, no endless discovery.

**Q: Can you work with our existing content team?**
A: Yes — and this is often the most effective model. I design the strategy and frameworks, your team executes the content, and I review and optimize. It keeps costs lower and builds internal capability at the same time.

**Q: What tools do you use?**
A: Ahrefs, SEMrush, Screaming Frog, Sitebulb, Google Search Console, GA4, Looker Studio, Clearscope, Surfer, Frase, Python, n8n, Make, and a growing stack of AI tools. I'll work with whatever you already have where possible.

**Q: How do I know if I'm ready to work with you?**
A: If you're a SaaS founder who has tried SEO and it hasn't worked, or you're starting fresh and want to do it right from the beginning — you're ready. If you're looking for someone to just publish blogs and hope for the best, I'm probably not the right fit.
